About the MinSU Calapan City Campus Bulletin

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

The MinSU Calapan City Campus Bulletin is a student-developed website created to provide a centralized and organized space for campus announcements, events, academic reminders, and other information relevant to students of Mindoro State University  -  Calapan City Campus.

Purpose

This website is designed to make important campus information easier to find and access. Instead of mixing different types of information in one place, the bulletin organizes content into categories such as announcements and events.

What You Can Find Here

• University and campus announcements
• Upcoming campus events and activities
• Enrollment and registration reminders
• Academic calendar reminders
• Important notices for students

Important Notice

This website is created as part of a student CMS project and is not an official Mindoro State University website or official university communication channel. Information published here for demonstration purposes should not be treated as an official university announcement.

Students should always refer to the official communication channels of Mindoro State University for confirmed announcements, schedules, policies, and other university information.
