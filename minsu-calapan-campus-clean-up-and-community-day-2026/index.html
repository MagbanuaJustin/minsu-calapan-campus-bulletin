Students are invited to participate in the MinSU Calapan Campus Clean-Up and Community Day 2026, a campus activity focused on maintaining a clean, organized, and welcoming university environment.

Planned Activities

• Campus and classroom clean-up
• Proper waste collection and segregation

Why Campus Clean-Up and Community Participation Matter

A clean and organized campus helps create a more comfortable and welcoming environment for students, faculty, staff, and visitors. Campus cleanliness is not only about improving the appearance of the university; it also encourages students to become more responsible for the spaces they use every day.

Through activities such as the MinSU Calapan Campus Clean-Up and Community Day 2026, students can work together while developing teamwork, cooperation, discipline, and environmental awareness. Simple actions such as properly disposing of waste, separating recyclable materials, and keeping classrooms and outdoor areas clean can contribute to maintaining a better campus environment.

Environmental Responsibility

Students can help protect the campus environment by practicing responsible waste management and encouraging others to do the same. Participants should observe proper waste segregation and place collected materials in their appropriate disposal areas.

Students are encouraged to:

• Dispose of waste in designated containers.
• Separate recyclable and non-recyclable materials when appropriate.
• Avoid leaving waste in classrooms, hallways, gardens, and open spaces.
• Reduce unnecessary use of disposable materials when possible.
• Respect plants, landscaped areas, and other campus facilities.
• Encourage classmates and friends to maintain clean surroundings.

Working Together as a Campus Community

The activity also provides an opportunity for students to cooperate toward a shared goal. Different classes, student organizations, and volunteers may work together in assigned areas while following the instructions of activity coordinators.

Participating in a community activity can help students understand that maintaining the university environment is a shared responsibility. Even small contributions become meaningful when students work together.

After the Clean-Up

Keeping the campus clean should continue even after the activity has ended. Students can apply the same practices during regular school days by properly disposing of their own waste, keeping classrooms organized, and reminding others to care for shared spaces.

The goal is not simply to have a clean campus for one day, but to encourage habits that support a cleaner, greener, and more responsible university community.

Building Lasting Environmental Habits

Campus cleanliness becomes more effective when responsible practices continue beyond organized clean-up activities. Students can contribute to a cleaner university environment every day by developing simple habits that reduce waste and help protect shared campus spaces.

Environmental responsibility can begin with small everyday actions. Students can properly dispose of their waste after eating, keep classrooms and study areas clean, reduce unnecessary disposable materials, and use available waste segregation bins correctly. These practices can help prevent litter from accumulating and encourage others to become more mindful of their surroundings.

Simple Habits Students Can Practice

• Bring a reusable water bottle or container when possible.
• Use designated waste bins and follow proper waste segregation.
• Avoid leaving food containers, paper, and other waste in classrooms.
• Keep personal study and activity areas organized before leaving.
• Reduce unnecessary use of single-use materials when practical.
• Respect campus plants, gardens, facilities, and other shared spaces.
• Encourage classmates and friends to practice responsible waste disposal.

Creating a Culture of Responsibility

Maintaining a clean campus is a shared responsibility. When students consistently practice good environmental habits, these actions can encourage classmates and other members of the campus community to do the same.

Clean-up activities can therefore serve as more than one-day events. They can remind students that protecting the campus environment requires continuous participation, cooperation, and personal responsibility.

By turning simple actions into everyday habits, students can help maintain a campus that is cleaner, greener, organized, and welcoming for everyone.
