Making the Most of Student Orientation

Student orientation can help students become more comfortable with their new academic environment. Beyond learning where facilities are located, orientation gives students an opportunity to understand university procedures, available services, academic expectations, and the different resources they may use throughout the academic year.

Get Familiar With the Campus

Students are encouraged to become familiar with important areas around the campus. Knowing where essential offices and facilities are located can make it easier to ask for assistance and complete university-related transactions when needed.

During orientation, students can take note of locations such as:

• Classrooms and academic buildings
• Administrative and student service offices
• Library and study areas
• Campus activity areas and facilities
• Offices where students can ask about academic concerns
• Emergency exits and designated safety areas
• Other facilities introduced during the orientation

Learn About Student Services and Resources

Orientation can also introduce students to services that may support them during their studies. Students should pay attention to presentations about academic procedures, student organizations, campus resources, and other forms of assistance available within the university.

When something is unclear, students should take the opportunity to ask questions or determine which university office they should approach for additional information.

Connect With the Campus Community

For new students, orientation can be an opportunity to meet classmates and become more familiar with faculty, staff, student leaders, and other members of the campus community.

Students are encouraged to participate respectfully in orientation activities, communicate with fellow students, and become involved in appropriate campus activities. Building positive connections can help students adjust to university life and develop teamwork and communication skills.

Start the Academic Year Prepared

Students can use what they learn during orientation to prepare for the beginning of classes. They should keep important instructions, schedules, contact information, and other materials provided during the activity for future reference.

Orientation is the beginning of becoming familiar with university life. Explore the campus, learn about available resources, ask questions, meet your campus community, and begin the academic year prepared.
